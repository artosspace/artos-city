HOW TO
PLAY

The goal

Find the 10 lost film rolls around the city (yellow dots on the map). Take them to the Film Lab (teal ring) to develop them. Every roll developed is a level up, and levels unlock new hairstyles.

The studio

The four charcoal buildings with coral neon signs are Artos Studios: Work, Services, About and Book. Step onto the coral ring in front of one to open that page.

The Rooftop

Look for the purple door and the pink ROOFTOP sign near the start (purple on the map). Walk in to go up to the rooftop club: light-up dance floor, a DJ, and a crowd dancing to whatever's playing. Stand still and you'll dance. Press A (or E) to head back down.

Game Store

Yellow door near the start (yellow on the map). Inside, the ARTOS STRIKE cabinet takes you to a war zone: the city's on fire and grey robots run the streets. Grab the heavy machine gun (H), rocket launcher (R), shield (S), grenades (G) and extra lives from crates, jump in the Artos tank, then take down the Grey Mech. On a phone: B fires (hold), A jumps, the GRENADE button throws, up on the D-pad aims up. On a keyboard: J fire, K or Space jump, L grenade, W aim up.

Artos Supply

The clothing store (green door). Change your fit, add a cap or beanie in any colour, and switch your hair colour.

Camera, jobs and HQ

Tap CAM (or C) to shoot a photo anywhere. Photos pin to the Polaroid wall at Artos HQ (white door), where you can save them. Tap JOBS (or J) for client briefs: shoot a location (some need golden hour, night or rain) or rush a delivery against the clock. Jobs pay cash and rep. Spend it at Camera Exchange (pink door) on a camcorder, Mamiya 7, lighting kit, Blackmagic 6K and a cinema rig. New cameras add new photo looks; the rig unlocks music video shoots at HQ, which record a clip you can save.

Radio, barber and emotes

Tap RADIO (or R) to flick between Drazy Radio, Dhey Originals, Blue Note Jazz, Boom Bap FM and Slow Jams. Fresh Cuts (red door) sells new haircuts. EMOTE (or keys 1 to 4) to wave, dance, flash or hype, and friends in your city see it.

The phone

Tap 📱 Phone for the map, camera, jobs, radio, emotes, chat and settings.

DJ Quin B and the decks

Walk into the club and DJ Quin B's mix kicks in from a random point in the set; the city radio stops until you leave. There's a bar on the left (first drink's on the house). Use the dance buttons (or keys 1 to 5) for the two-step, the Candy (electric slide), Milly Rock, wind and Shmoney. Walk up to the DJ booth and press A (or tap Decks) to mix on two decks: EASY mode has one-tap MIX IN, scratching and effect pads; PRO mode adds tempo, EQ, cue points, the crossfader and filter.

Travel

At the airport you can fly to New York, Kingston, Port of Spain, Tokyo or Paris. Each city has its own streets, colours and vibe, and the islands have beaches and dolphins.

Riding together

In a shared city, walk up to a friend's car and press A to ride as a passenger. Press B (or Q) to ask to drive, and they can hand you the keys with LET THEM DRIVE. Tap Chat on the phone (or Enter) to talk.

More places

The Note (latte-coloured door) is a café where the barista only takes orders in French. Get a few right for a surprise. The orange door is a rooftop basketball court: press A to shoot. The big screen near the club plays DJ Quin B's mix when you walk past. You can also fly to London (Brixton).

Ice Box

The jewellery store (icy blue door) sells grillz and chains.

Car meet

Drive to the orange ring by the airport car park and stop on it. The whips pull up.

Getting around

Tap GPS (or press G) and pick a place: a line on the road shows you the way. Want a lift? Walk up to any yellow cab and press A, or use the taxi rank (yellow ring near the start), pick a stop and the driver takes you there. Every place has an EXIT TO STREET button.

Airport

The airport is in the far corner (blue on the map). Walk into the terminal and board the Artos jet, hold GAS down the runway to take off, fly anywhere over the city, then come back over the runway and ease off to land.

Vehicles

Grab anything on the road: saloons, motorbikes (quick and nippy), sports cars (fastest), the boxy 4x4, or a big lorry (slow and heavy). Driving burns fuel, shown next to your speed. When it runs low, pull into a petrol station (orange on the map) and stop by the pumps to fill up.

On a phone (ARTOS·BOY)

On foot: D-pad to walk, A to get in a car, B to say something.
In a car: drag the steering wheel to turn, hold GAS or BRAKE (hold brake to reverse), tap the middle of the wheel to honk, EXIT CAR to get out.
START opens the menu, SELECT mutes the music. Left or right handed? Turn on Swap control sides in the menu.

On a keyboard

WASD or arrows to move or drive · E get in or out · T talk · H horn · M mute music · N next track · P previous track · Shift run · Esc menu

Weather and time

The sun moves through a full day every 8 minutes: bright mornings, orange sunsets and neon nights, with shadows that move with the sun. The weather changes every minute or so: clear, rain, autumn leaves, snow, spring blossom and summer. Bad weather keeps people indoors, and in the rain they bring umbrellas. You can lock a season in the menu.

Play with friends

Host a city to get a 4-letter code, and friends join with it. This works once the game is on its own website.
